# Pyramid App Calculation Rules and Question Guide

**Date:** 2026-07-13  
**Purpose:** Reference document for a ChatGPT Project used to understand and review automatic simulation results from the Pyramid App.

---

## 1. Purpose of This Document

Use this document to answer questions such as:

- Why was this value placed for this member on this date?
- Why was PVP 100 assigned to the parent member rather than the descendant member?
- Why were the same total values distributed across different members or dates?
- What changes if a value is removed or moved to another date?
- Does the automatic simulation result follow the current company rules?

This document describes **only the current rules used for new plans**. Historical calculation practices must not be used as the decision standard for current plans.

---

## 2. Rules for ChatGPT When Answering Questions

1. Treat this document as the primary reference for current calculation rules.
2. Do not invent or infer numbers that are not visible in the screenshot or supplied data.
3. Always distinguish direct allocations from values propagated from descendant organizations.
4. Do not analyze one cell in isolation. Consider the member, descendants, ancestors, previous-day balances, same-day settlement, carry, reset, qualification PVP, and half-month targets.
5. When a screenshot is insufficient, ask only for the specific missing values or screens needed.
6. If an explanation appears to conflict with the app result, first confirm the exact inputs and calculation trace used by the app.
7. Distinguish a **verified usable plan** from a **mathematically proven optimum**.
8. Do not state an uncertain interpretation as an official company rule.
9. Explain the answer in plain Korean unless the user explicitly requests another language.
10. Preserve the English technical terms and field names used in this document when precision matters.

## 3. Core Terms

### 3.1 Direct Allocation

A value entered directly for a specific member in the plan:

- `PVP`
- direct `SELF_LEFT`
- direct `SELF_RIGHT`

When a direction is connected to a child organization, that direction receives propagated organization performance. A direct self-allocation is not entered into the connected `CHILD` direction.

### 3.2 Organization Performance

Values generated by a descendant member and that member's organization propagate upward to the corresponding side of each ancestor.

The same direct allocation may affect several ancestors as propagated organization performance. However, the direct cost is counted only once, at the original allocation cell.

### 3.3 Daily Balance

The daily PVP, left, and right balances used in the daily commission calculation.

- If no commission settlement occurs, remaining balances may carry to the next date.
- If a commission settlement occurs, the daily balances reset according to the official calculation rules.
- The app's calculation trace is the authority for the exact carry and reset result.

### 3.4 Qualification PVP

The cumulative PVP used to determine whether a member is eligible for a normal commission.

```text
qualificationPvp(member, date)
= openingQualificationPvp(member)
+ cumulative direct new PVP allocated to that member through the date, inclusive
```

Qualification PVP does not reset when a daily commission settlement occurs.

### 3.5 Daily PVP Balance

The PVP balance used in the daily left/right commission calculation. It may be applied to one side and may reset after settlement.

This is not the same as cumulative qualification PVP.

### 3.6 Half-Month PVP Assessment

The PVP value used to determine whether the member's selected half-month PVP target has been met.

Qualification PVP, daily PVP balance, and half-month PVP assessment have different meanings and must not be treated as one interchangeable value.

## 4. Current Calculation Rules

### 4.1 Half-Month Planning

A plan covers either the first half or the second half of a month.

Every member must meet:

- the selected personal half-month PVP target;
- assessed half-month left performance of at least 2,500;
- assessed half-month right performance of at least 2,500.

A target is satisfied when the assessed value is equal to or greater than the required value.

### 4.2 Sunday Handling

Sunday is a `SKIP_NO_INPUT` date.

- All direct PVP, left, and right allocations must be zero on Sunday.
- Dates are interpreted as business date-only values.
- Sunday must not change because the browser is used in Korea, Brazil, or another timezone.

### 4.3 PVP 300 Qualification

The automatic plan must not trigger a normal commission while a member's cumulative qualification PVP is below 300.

Direct PVP allocated on the same date is added before that date's qualification gate is checked.

Example:

```text
Opening qualification PVP: 33
Same-date new PVP: 267
Qualification PVP: 300
Result: A normal commission may occur on that date.
```

```text
Opening qualification PVP: 33
Same-date new PVP: 266
Qualification PVP: 299
Result: A normal commission must not occur on that date.
```

One-sided performance may accumulate below qualification when it does not trigger a commission settlement.

### 4.4 PVP Is Applied to the Smaller Side

Under the current rule, a member's daily PVP is applied to the smaller of the left and right sides.

Example:

```text
PVP 100
Left 600
Right 700
```

PVP 100 is applied to the smaller side:

```text
Assessed left 700
Assessed right 700
```

This may complete a 700-tier commission.

When left and right are equal, the app follows the official tie-left rule.

### 4.5 PVP 100 Has No Fixed Preferred Owner or Position

Under the historical rule, PVP could not be added to the smaller side. A common operating practice was therefore to place PVP 100 on a descendant whose left and right sides were 300 and 300, producing a subtree total of 700 for an ancestor.

Under the current rule, PVP can be applied to the member's smaller side. Therefore, placing the same PVP 100 directly on an ancestor may sometimes be more efficient.

Example A:

```text
Descendant:
PVP 100 / Left 300 / Right 300
Subtree total propagated upward: 700
```

Example B:

```text
Descendant:
PVP 0 / Left 300 / Right 300
Subtree total propagated upward: 600

Ancestor:
Direct PVP 100
Smaller side 600 + PVP 100
Result: 700
```

The optimizer must not prefer PVP 100 merely because it is 100, and it must not force PVP 100 onto a particular member.

The best placement depends on the whole-plan result:

- total direct new PV;
- each member's personal PVP target;
- qualification PVP;
- same-day commission completion;
- carry and reset effects;
- discarded excess;
- effects on multiple ancestors;
- whether the same PVP allocation solves more than one requirement.

### 4.6 Daily Calculation Order

For each date, calculate in this conceptual order:

1. Load balances carried from the previous date.
2. Add same-date direct allocations and descendant organization performance.
3. Apply daily PVP to the smaller side.
4. Check cumulative qualification PVP.
5. Determine whether an official commission tier is reached.
6. If no settlement occurs, carry the remaining balances according to the rules.
7. If settlement occurs, apply the official reset and carry consequences.

Use the app's calculation engine and trace for exact values.

### 4.7 Discarded Excess

On a valid full-commission day:

```text
discardedExcessPv
= preSettlement.pvp
+ preSettlement.left
+ preSettlement.right
- 2 × commissionTier
```

This measures PV erased above the minimum required for the achieved commission tier.

Example:

```text
PVP 500 / Left 0 / Right 300
```

If PVP is applied to the smaller side and a 300-tier commission occurs:

```text
Total pre-settlement PV: 800
PV required for the 300 tier: 600
Discarded excess: 200
```

Qualification PVP surplus is different. Cumulative qualification PVP does not reset merely because a daily settlement occurs.

## 5. How the Automatic Simulation Chooses a Plan

The objectives are applied in strict order. A lower-priority preference must never worsen a higher-priority result.

### Priority 1: Minimize Total Direct New PV

Minimize the sum of all direct PVP, direct `SELF_LEFT`, and direct `SELF_RIGHT` allocations while satisfying every hard target.

Propagated organization performance is not counted again as new cost.

### Priority 2: Minimize Total Discarded Excess

Among plans with the same minimum total direct new PV, prefer the plan that loses less PV during daily settlement.

### Priority 3: Improve Commission-Day Distribution for Target-700 Members

Only after priorities 1 and 2 are tied:

1. maximize the number of target-700 members who receive at least 8 qualification-valid commission days;
2. compare all target-700 members' commission-day counts in ascending order and prefer the more balanced distribution.

Do not add extra PV merely to create more commission days.

The total number of commission days may be displayed as a statistic, but it is not an additional tie-breaking objective after the full ascending day vector has already been compared.

### Priority 4: Prefer Communication-Friendly 100-PV Multiples

Only when all higher objectives are identical, prefer fewer non-zero direct cells whose value is not divisible by 100.

Examples such as 100, 200, and 300 are easier to communicate, but exact values such as 67, 153, or 241 remain valid whenever they reduce total PV or improve a higher-priority result.

### Priority 5: Avoid Unnecessary PVP Concentration

When all preceding metrics are tied, prefer the plan with the smaller maximum direct PVP cell.

This does not create a daily cap, and it does not make several PVP-100 cells inherently better than one PVP-200 cell.

### Priority 6: Deterministic Final Tie-Break

When all business results are identical, the app uses a stable date/member/field order to select one plan consistently.

This final tie-break does not mean the chosen plan has lower cost or better commissions. It only prevents equivalent results from changing between runs.

## 6. Result Statuses

### `OPTIMAL` — Minimum Confirmed

Use only when the optimizer has completed a valid proof that no better plan exists under the current model and rules.

### `TIME_LIMIT` with a Verified Candidate — Best Verified Plan Found So Far

The 30-minute search ended with a plan that passed the official calculation engine, but the proof that no better plan exists was not completed.

The plan is usable, but it must not be described as mathematically optimal.

### `TIME_LIMIT` with No Candidate

No verified usable plan was found within the 30-minute search.

This does not prove that no valid plan exists.

### `INFEASIBLE` — No Valid Plan Proven

Use only when the exact search has proven that the current targets and constraints cannot be satisfied.

### `CANCELLED`

The user stopped the search. The latest verified candidate may still be available.

### `FAILED`

A technical error or model-consistency error occurred. Do not present an unverified solver result as usable.

## 7. What a Screenshot Should Show

A screenshot may be used for questions. Whenever possible, include:

- member name;
- date;
- questioned cell: PVP, left, or right;
- previous-day ending balances;
- same-date direct allocations;
- organization performance received from descendants;
- values before and after PVP is applied;
- qualification PVP on that date;
- commission tier;
- post-settlement balances;
- half-month PVP and left/right target progress;
- relevant direct descendants and ancestor, when visible.

Multiple screenshots are acceptable when one screen cannot show the full context.

## 10. Information ChatGPT May Request

When the screenshots are insufficient, request only the relevant items:

- previous-date post-settlement PVP, left, and right balances;
- same-date performance by direct descendant;
- left/right values before and after PVP application;
- same-date qualification PVP;
- commission tier and post-settlement balances;
- selected half-month PVP target and current assessed PVP;
- half-month left/right target progress;
- same-date allocations for direct descendants;
- same-date result for the immediate ancestor;
- recalculation result after changing the questioned value to zero.

Without sufficient data, do not conclude that the app is wrong or that the allocation is inefficient.

---

## 11. Comparing an App Plan with an Actual Human Plan

Do not compare only whether the cells look alike. Compare:

1. whether every member meets the selected half-month PVP target;
2. whether every member meets assessed left and right targets of at least 2,500;
3. whether any commission occurs below qualification PVP 300;
4. total direct new PV;
5. total discarded excess;
6. target-700 commission-day distribution;
7. daily carry and reset;
8. whether each PVP allocation fills an actual shortage;
9. whether one allocation usefully contributes to several ancestors;
10. whether an irregular exact value is necessary to reduce a higher-priority metric.

Different-looking plans may both be valid. Explain the difference according to the objective priority order.

## 12. Scope

This document covers:

- the current PVP-to-smaller-side rule;
- half-month automatic planning;
- PVP 300 qualification;
- daily carry and reset;
- organization propagation;
- automatic-plan objective order;
- screenshot-based calculation questions.

This document does not cover:

- recalculating historical periods under the old rule;
- partial resimulation with completed dates locked;
- product combinations, prices, inventory, or Korean won cost;
- login, storage, or authorization;
- unsupported daily limits.

If the official company rule or the app's finalized calculation contract changes, update the authoritative rule document before relying on this guide.
